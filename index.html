    Spend-down withdrawal. The largest initial retirement withdrawal whose future amounts rise with inflation while ending net balance reaches $0 at end age. The schedule is anchored at retirement and uses portfolio growth and margin debt only.

    How tax is modeled. Taxable gains = gross withdrawal × gains %. Federal tax applies above the inflated 0% LTCG bound and standard deduction. State tax applies to all taxable gains without federal offsets. Gross withdrawal is solved so its after-tax proceeds exactly equal net needed.
